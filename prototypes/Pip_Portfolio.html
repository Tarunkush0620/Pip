Who is Pip?

Pip is a curious, sleepy, a little clumsy baby with a smooth round skull face and huge glowing eyes. Pip designs the dreams, lullabies, nightlights and bedtime stories that help the neighbourhood’s little ghosts and creatures fall asleep. Pip giggles at everything and hates silence.
